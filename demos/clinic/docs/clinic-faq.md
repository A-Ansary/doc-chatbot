# Bright Smile Dental Clinic - Frequently Asked Questions

## About us
Bright Smile Dental Clinic is a family dental clinic with 4 dentists, including a specialist in orthodontics and a children's dentist.
Address: 120 Harbor Street, Suite 4, Springfield. Free parking behind the building.
Phone: (555) 010-2030. Email: hello@brightsmile.example

## Opening hours
- Monday to Thursday: 9:00 AM - 7:00 PM
- Friday: 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM
- Saturday: 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM
- Sunday: closed
For dental emergencies outside opening hours, call our emergency line: (555) 010-9999.

## Services and prices
- Check-up and consultation: $60
- Cleaning and polishing: $95
- Tooth-coloured filling: from $150
- Teeth whitening (in-clinic): $350
- Invisalign / clear aligners: free first consultation, treatment from $2,900
- Children's check-up (under 12): $45
- Root canal treatment: from $700
Prices are estimates; the dentist confirms the final price after examination.

## Insurance and payment
We accept most major dental insurance plans, including Delta Dental, Cigna and MetLife. We also accept cash, cards and Apple Pay.
Payment plans are available for treatments over $1,000 (up to 12 months, no interest).

## Appointments
New patients are welcome. Most check-ups are available within 3 days.
Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit and bring your insurance card and ID.
Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the appointment; late cancellations may be charged $25.

## Children
We see children from age 2. Our children's dentist, Dr. Lina Park, works Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays.

## Languages
Our team speaks English, Spanish and Arabic.
