# Iron Zone Gym - Frequently Asked Questions

## Opening hours
We are open Saturday to Thursday from 6:00 AM to 12:00 AM, and Friday from 2:00 PM to 11:00 PM.

## Memberships and prices
- Monthly membership: 900 EGP
- 3 months: 2,400 EGP
- 12 months: 8,000 EGP
- Students get 15% off any membership with a valid university ID.

## Boxing and wrestling classes
Boxing classes run Saturday, Monday and Wednesday at 7:00 PM. Wrestling classes run Sunday and Tuesday at 8:00 PM.
Classes are included in the 3-month and 12-month memberships; monthly members pay 300 EGP extra.

## Personal training
Personal training sessions cost 400 EGP per session, or 3,500 EGP for a package of 10 sessions.

## Freezing and cancellation
Members on 3-month or 12-month plans can freeze their membership once for up to 30 days.
Memberships are non-refundable after the first 7 days.

## Location and parking
We are in Sheikh Zayed, next to the main mall. Free parking is available for members.
